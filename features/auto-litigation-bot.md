# Auto-Litigation Bot

This feature will involve creating a bot that can automatically file GDPR violation claims, track settlement progress, and distribute compensation to users.

## Scope of Work

- Research legal requirements and procedures for filing GDPR violation claims.
- Develop a system for identifying and documenting GDPR violations.
- Integrate with legal APIs or platforms for filing claims (if available).
- Develop a system for tracking the status of filed claims.
- Implement a secure mechanism for distributing compensation to users.
- Ensure compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.
- Write comprehensive tests.
