Starred pages in the resource menus of applications, services, databases, and servers

- Hover a menu page and click its star to pin it in a `Starred` group at the top of the menu. Drag the starred pages, or press Alt+Up/Down, to reorder them. The command palette can star the current page and open starred pages. Stars are saved per user and apply to every resource of the same type
