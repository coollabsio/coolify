Logs of removed containers on the `Logs` page, saved by Sentinel on the server

- The `Container` list on the `Logs` page shows the saved logs of removed containers, newest first
- Sentinel keeps the logs for 7 days and at most 1 GB on each server
- The `Keep logs of removed containers` setting on the `Sentinel` page of a server is on by default. When you turn it off, the saved logs are deleted
