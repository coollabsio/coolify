# Viewsjs

## One experimental badge and one experimental icon
Mark a feature that is not stable yet with `<x-experimental-badge />` in the top right of its page, and its sidebar item with `<x-experimental-icon />`. Use the icon only in sidebar items. Never add another badge, icon, or variant. Svelte canvases use `ExperimentalBadge.svelte`.
